# PaperWorkShell licensing and distribution

Publisher and maintainer: **Janne Vuorela**. The official PaperWorkShell distribution is free
for all users, including commercial users. Product use requires no account, registration,
email address, activation, subscription or licence key.

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) applies to source code and other material that PaperWorkShell owns
or has authority to license under MIT. It does not relicense third-party material, assets,
vendored files, reviewed upstream notices, or contributions for which that authority has not
been established. Preserve existing copyright/contributor notices and any file-specific terms.

Third-party components retain their own licences and obligations. Exact candidate inventories,
SBOMs, unchanged notices, corresponding source and replacement/relinking instructions identify
the shipped components. In particular, MIT does not replace Qt/LGPL rights or grant rights to
Microsoft runtime code. Component-specific terms must remain scoped to those components and
must not restrict rights granted by MIT or LGPL elsewhere.

Documents and document-derived data stay local. PaperWorkShell has no document upload,
telemetry or automatic crash-report submission. An internal unsigned installer is a local
engineering artifact; it is not a publicly trusted or approved public release. Public
distribution still requires resolved component rights, approved trusted signatures and the
applicable release qualification. No licence text or notice constitutes that qualification.
