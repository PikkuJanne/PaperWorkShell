## Confirmed OCR native-closure blocker

The selected MSYS2 UCRT64 Tesseract closure has 30 packages and 39 selected files. Offline
inspection verified their recorded archive and selected-member byte counts and SHA-256 values.
The jbigkit package contains GPL version 2 license text and declares `spdx:GPL-2.0` in its package
metadata. Its selected `bin/libjbig-0.dll` is therefore not covered merely by Tesseract's
Apache-2.0 label.

| Evidence | Bytes | SHA-256 |
|---|---:|---|
| `mingw-w64-ucrt-x86_64-jbigkit-2.1-5-any.pkg.tar.zst` | 74962 | `334e156affd0052de8ae549ec7044d3b2cdb1f0c72569659619fb8e820e5d878` |
| Archive `.PKGINFO` | 502 | `82789498b6237b5fdedb5eaa854d0bb139f4688b4252087dc607b46512932634` |
| Archive `ucrt64/share/licenses/jbigkit/COPYING` | 17982 | `91df39d1816bfb17a4dda2d3d2c83b1f6f2d38d53e53e41e8f97ad5ac46a0cad` |
| Selected `bin/libjbig-0.dll` | 66242 | `6d1657e08318630738fb0841ee28bbe7272b41b3b2ef93adaa228c4cd10092c6` |

Normal PE imports in the exact selected bytes establish these loader dependencies:

```text
tesseract.exe -> libtiff-6.dll -> libjbig-0.dll
tesseract.exe -> libtesseract-5.5.dll -> libleptonica-6.dll -> libtiff-6.dll
```

The inspected binaries have no delay-import table. The pinned Tesseract executable is
`da07f581278e934f8728c406ce426e0664e3be8e90116685823cb301ba4fd6db`; libtiff is
`ce0eb686a9b03a8586d7d1f833fadcbe67ad2f6ff8538c3f431d4edb072f935d`.
Deleting JBIG breaks the Windows loader before an input-format restriction can help. Merely
excluding TIFF from the supported user inputs cannot close this issue.

The [JBIG-KIT author's licensing page](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/jbigkit/) describes GPL,
commercial, and dependency-license routes. Its discussion of unreachable codec paths is not
treated as an approved license for this bundled DLL. No commercial or dependency-license rights
have been supplied. The project's [distribution policy](dependency-policy.md#distribution-changing-dependencies)
and PWS-D034 prohibit silently changing the selected distribution terms. MSYS2 itself says its
[package license metadata](https://www.msys2.org/license/) is best-effort; metadata labels alone
cannot close file-specific obligations.

Other mixed package labels require per-file review: GCC runtime exceptions, gettext's LGPL
library versus GPL programs, libiconv, libidn2, libunistring, lz4, xz, and zstd. These are not all
declared additional blockers; their selected file licenses and required source/notices still
need resolution. An archive-level GPL label must neither silently approve a GPL library nor
incorrectly reject a library whose actual alternative or runtime exception is acceptable.

lxml wheel's `RNG2Schtrn.xsl` and `XSD2Schtrn.xsl` have unresolved redistribution rights and that

## Alternatives investigated and required action

- **Remove JBIG alone:** rejected; the exact libtiff DLL requires it at load time.
- **Use Pillow's TIFF implementation:** unsuitable as a DLL replacement. Pillow embeds TIFF
  4.7.1 statically in its extension; it supplies no corresponding `libtiff-6.dll`.
- **Rename a different vendor's libtiff:** not accepted as ABI or provenance evidence. A different
  toolchain, export surface, codec dependencies, and Windows-I/O patches require a complete
  adapter/runtime admission review.
- **Build libtiff 4.7.2 without JBIG:** a technically available ordinary engineering alternative;
  upstream supports disabling the codec. It needs a pinned source/toolchain/recipe, complete
  import/ABI review, new artifact hashes, a new reviewed native closure, corresponding notices,
  and worker/OCR/security/resource regression evidence. No such replacement is built or approved
  by this audit. Missing local compilers alone are not declared a contractual blocker.
- **Obtain rights for the selected DLL:** needs maintainer-owned license terms/clearance, or the
  explicit frozen-decision procedure if the intended distribution terms change.
- **Exclude the lxml resources:** propose and prove a minimal package subset, then obtain the
  maintainer/legal review expressly required by the existing policy. Do not rename this proposal
  as completed clearance.

The external gate is the maintainer/legal rights or packaging determination for the exact lxml
resource-exclusion proposal above (or supplied rights for those resources). Separately, the
current JBIG route must be replaced through a reviewed no-JBIG build or closed through supplied
rights. The rebuild is ordinary engineering and can preserve the frozen distribution policy;
its technical feasibility is not a reason to invent license clearance. The affected decisions
are PWS-D034 and PWS-D042; no frozen decision was edited.
